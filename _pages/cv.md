# Research Experience

**Postdoctoral Scientist**
Gulbenkian Institute for Molecular Medicine (GIMM), Lisbon, Portugal | Marco Fumasoni Lab
*January 2023 – June 2026*

* Led a 3.5-year project on **cell size adaptation in budding yeast**, combining experimental evolution, genetic engineering and quantitative phenotyping.
* Analysed **complex genomic datasets in Python** to identify mutations associated with cellular adaptation and stable cell-size reduction.
* Integrated genomic, microscopy and flow-cytometry data to investigate genotype–phenotype relationships.

**PhD Researcher**
International Max Planck Research School for Evolutionary Biology & Kiel University, Germany | Tal Dagan Lab
*October 2018 – December 2022*

* Led a 4-year project on **genetic drift, plasmid inheritance and bacterial adaptation**, with implications for antibiotic resistance spread.
* Developed a **novel experimental system** to control plasmid allele frequency at single-cell and population levels.
* Collaborated with mathematicians to combine experimental data with mathematical modelling and quantitative analysis.
