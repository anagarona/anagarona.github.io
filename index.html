ABOUT

Research & expertise

My research combines experimental and computational approaches to investigate how genetic variation shapes biological phenotypes and evolutionary processes.

During my postdoctoral research, I investigated cell size adaptation in budding yeast, integrating experimental evolution, genetic engineering, quantitative phenotyping and genomic data analysis.

During my PhD, I studied genetic drift, plasmid inheritance and bacterial adaptation, combining experimental approaches with mathematical modelling and quantitative analysis.

Genomic and NGS data analysis, population genetics, phylogenetics and evolutionary genomics.

Computational Biology
